HOW TO APPLY

Step 1 — Choose your course
Browse programmes or use the Course Comparison tool.
Step 2 — Check entry requirements
Make sure you meet the MQF and subject requirements.
Step 3 — Prepare documents
Examples: ID card, Certificates, Portfolio, Application form
Step 4 — Submit your application
Apply online via the admissions portal.
Step 5 — Follow-up & results
Check email for updates, interviews, or missing documents.
